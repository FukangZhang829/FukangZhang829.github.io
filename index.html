// About

Turning Industrial Problems into Intelligent Systems

Engineer · Researcher · Builder — a quick snapshot of who I am.

I'm Fukang Zhang (张福康), a master's student at Beihang University's National Superior College for Engineers (Electronic Information · Key Software), and an AI Engineer at AVIC Digital (AVIC Information Technology Center). My research focuses on industrial large models & agents, production scheduling optimization, intelligent manufacturing, graph neural networks and reinforcement learning.

I work at the intersection of frontier AI and real manufacturing — building LLM agents that schedulers actually use, tuning GNN models that beat classical priority rules, and shipping full-stack systems from data annotation platforms to federated trading marketplaces.

I was the student representative at the first graduate congress of Shenyuan College, served as head of the Physical & Mental Development Department of the graduate union, and am the publicity committee member of my party branch — with 200+ hours of volunteer service.
